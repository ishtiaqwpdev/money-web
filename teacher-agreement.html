1. Independent Contractor Relationship

The instructor acknowledges and agrees that they are engaged as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, joint venturer, or agent of Gospel Music Mastery or Caldwell International Consultants LLC. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship. The instructor retains the right to control the manner and means of providing instructional services, subject to platform guidelines and applicable law.

2. Taxes and Benefits

The instructor is solely responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes arising from compensation received through the platform. No employee benefits—including but not limited to health insurance, paid leave, unemployment insurance, or retirement contributions—are provided to the instructor under this agreement.

3. Professional Conduct

The instructor shall conduct themselves professionally and respectfully at all times when interacting with students, parents/guardians, platform staff, and other instructors. Harassment, discrimination, abusive language, or conduct that harms the reputation of Gospel Music Mastery is strictly prohibited and may result in immediate removal from the platform.

4. Student Safety and Communication

The instructor is responsible for maintaining a safe lesson environment (online or otherwise) and for appropriate, professional communication with students and guardians. The instructor shall follow platform policies regarding lesson settings, privacy, recording, and communication channels, and shall promptly report safety concerns as required.

5. Legal Authorization

The instructor represents that they are legally authorized to provide music instruction in the jurisdictions where they offer services, and that they will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and licensing requirements related to their instructional activities.

6. Background and Misconduct Disclosure

Where legally required or requested by the company, the instructor agrees to disclose relevant criminal history, misconduct findings, or other background information that may reasonably affect student safety or platform integrity. Failure to disclose required information may result in denial or termination of instructor status.

7. Copyright and Intellectual Property

The instructor may not use, reproduce, distribute, or perform copyrighted material without proper permission, license, or other lawful authorization. The instructor remains responsible for obtaining necessary rights for lesson materials they provide. Platform branding, content, and systems remain the property of Gospel Music Mastery / Caldwell International Consultants LLC.

8. Technology and Online Lessons

The instructor is responsible for maintaining suitable technology, internet connectivity, and a professional online teaching environment for virtual lessons. The company is not liable for interruptions caused by the instructor’s equipment, network, or third-party tools outside the company’s control.

9. Bookings, Hours, and Income

Gospel Music Mastery does not guarantee students, lesson hours, bookings, or any minimum income. Demand may vary. Compensation, if any, is based on completed and confirmed lessons according to then-current platform terms and schedules.

10. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Gospel Music Mastery and Caldwell International Consultants LLC shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages arising from the instructor’s services or use of the platform. The instructor agrees to perform services at their own professional risk, subject to applicable law.

11. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a dispute related to this agreement or platform participation, the parties agree to first attempt resolution through good-faith written communication. If unresolved, further remedies may be pursued as permitted under governing law and any additional platform policies.

12. Governing Law

This agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, without regard to conflict-of-law principles, except where mandatory local law requires otherwise.

13. Electronic Acceptance

By drawing an electronic signature, confirming understanding, and submitting this form, the instructor acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Teacher Independent Contractor & Liability Agreement. Electronic submission constitutes acceptance equivalent to a handwritten signature for demo and frontend purposes only.
