1. Enrollment Terms

By enrolling with Gospel Music Mastery, the student joins an online music education platform for lessons, learning resources, and related instructional services. Enrollment confirms the student’s intent to participate according to platform guidelines and applicable policies.

2. Lesson Participation

Students should attend scheduled lessons on time, prepare as requested by their instructor, and communicate professionally with teachers and support staff. Missed lessons without proper notice may be treated according to the cancellation policy below.

3. Payments and Fees

Students are responsible for paying lesson fees and any applicable platform charges by the stated due dates. Pricing, billing cycles, and accepted payment methods are shown at checkout or in the student account. Unpaid balances may limit booking or lesson access.

4. Cancellation Policy

Students should cancel or reschedule lessons within the notice period published on the platform (demo placeholder: typically 24 hours before the lesson). Late cancellations or no-shows may result in forfeiture of the lesson fee according to the then-current policy.

5. Student Conduct

Students agree to treat instructors, staff, and other learners with respect. Harassment, abusive language, discrimination, or disruptive behavior is not permitted and may result in suspension or termination of enrollment.

6. Online Learning Responsibility

Students are responsible for maintaining a suitable internet connection, device, and quiet learning space for online lessons. Gospel Music Mastery is not liable for interruptions caused by the student’s equipment, network, or third-party tools outside the platform’s control.

7. Privacy and Account Security

Students must keep login credentials confidential and promptly notify the platform of unauthorized account access. Students should not share personal account details or allow others to attend billed lessons under their account without permission.

8. Intellectual Property
